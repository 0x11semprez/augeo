# Business impact

OGF's Maison Funéraire du Mont Valérien (Nanterre) receives about **2,000 deceased a year** — one devis per deceased, generated by a team of 5. That works out to ~5.5 devis/day across the team on average (the site is open year-round), a little above the "one each per day" rule of thumb, which is close enough for staffing purposes but not the number to compute costs from — 2,000/year is the measured, reliable one. Annual revenue tied to this activity is in the €8M–€11M range.

## The manual process, before augeo

Each devis meant re-typing the same information by hand and hoping the arithmetic was right:

1. Copy the deceased's and family's info from the internal software into a document, by hand
2. Compute the prestations total by hand
3. Print it
4. Scan the printed/signed document
5. Print a second copy for billing
6. File the paper copy
7. Write an email to the funeral operator by hand and attach the scan

Seven manual steps, repeated ~2,000 times a year, with real error exposure at step 2.

## With augeo

1. Fill in the deceased's/family's info once, tick the prestations rendered
2. A correctly priced PDF is generated automatically, already named after the deceased
3. Outlook opens with the recipient, subject and body pre-filled — drag the PDF in (and still print or CC billing if a paper trail is wanted)

Two to three steps instead of seven, and the price is computed by the same template every time.

## What the friction was costing

All figures below use **2,000 devis/year** as the single volume basis (matching the deceased count, not the 5-people × 1/day approximation, which undercounts by about 9%), a wage of €15/hour, and 3 staff on duty per hour on average (based on observed shift overlap).

| | Before augeo | With augeo |
|---|---|---|
| Time per devis | 10 min | 3 min |
| Error rate | 15% | 5% |
| Creation time/year (2,000 × time/devis) | 333.3 h | 100 h |
| Correction time/year (errors × 20 min) | 100 h | 33.3 h |
| **Total non-productive time/year** | **433.3 h** | **133.3 h** |
| **Cost/year** (× 3 staff × €15/h) | **€19,500** | **€6,000** |

- **Net savings: €13,500/year**, or **€135,000 over 10 years** (roughly how long the manual process has been in place) — reclaimed from paperwork and calculation-error cleanup back into time with families: admissions, follow-up, the things that actually retain them and bring revenue in.
- augeo itself costs **€4.99/month** (the VPS; Vercel's frontend hosting is free) — about **€60/year**, or **€600 over 10 years**. Against €135,000 saved over the same period, that's roughly a **225x return**, and it doesn't count the (very real) reduction in family-facing error or the time no longer spent regularizing quotes under time pressure.

- These numbers supersede an earlier version of this doc that mixed two different volume bases (2,000/year for error counts, 1,825/year — 5 people × 1/day × 365 — for creation time) in the same table. Fixed here by using 2,000/year throughout.
- Your original note also had one figure that didn't reconcile: "~1h/day lost → 600h/year" (1h × 365 days is 365h, not 600h). It's dropped from this version since the table above is now computed directly rather than through that floor/day-rate shortcut.
- None of this prices the engineering time to build augeo itself, only its hosting cost — add that in if you need a fully loaded ROI for a formal business case.

## Ideas worth considering

- **Log corrections, not just generations.** If augeo flags when a devis is regenerated/edited after the fact, the 5% error-rate estimate above becomes a measured number within a few months instead of an estimate — strong, low-effort evidence for the next budget conversation.
- **Multi-site rollout.** This was scoped to one Maison Funéraire; if OGF operates others with a similar volume, the same €10K+/year saving multiplies per site for close to zero marginal engineering cost.
- **Push the error rate further down.** Front-end validation on postal code/city consistency and admission/departure date logic (some of this already exists) is cheap and would keep pulling the 5% down further.
- **Go fully paperless.** The backend already has to talk to a filesystem for xlsx/PDF generation; archiving each generated PDF there (or in object storage) would remove the "print for filing" branch entirely for teams willing to go all-digital.
- **A lightweight admin view.** Even a simple count of devis generated per month, shown to management, turns this from "the tool that makes the form" into visible proof of the time reclaimed — useful both internally and as a template for pitching augeo to other sites.
